Welcome to DKLABZ

How We Make Apps

We build applications for mobile, web, and desktop using a modern, scalable stack. We start with deep client collaboration, wireframe planning, and UI/UX prototyping. From there, we move into agile development with tools like React, Flutter, and Next.js, and integrate backend technologies like Node.js, Firebase, and AWS. Each application is tested rigorously for security, performance, and usability before deployment.

Our approach ensures rapid iteration and constant feedback loops, allowing us to pivot quickly and deliver precisely what users need—backed by analytics, AI, and automation where it counts most.

Hardware & Physical Products

We don’t just build apps—we bring ideas to life physically too. Our team designs and manufactures custom electronics, embedded systems, and even full device prototypes. From IoT gadgets to robotics and automation tools, we cover everything.

We utilize tools like Fusion 360 for 3D modeling, KiCad and Altium for PCB design, and rapid prototyping techniques including 3D printing and CNC milling. Each hardware project begins with a clear spec, schematic capture, PCB layout, and component sourcing. We then build and test the devices in-house.

Custom PCBs

Our custom PCB workflow is tailored to meet high-efficiency, low-noise design requirements. We work with clients to define circuit specifications, simulate functionality, and produce highly optimized board layouts. We handle everything from 2-layer sensor boards to 6-layer embedded microcontroller systems.

We manufacture using trusted partners and perform in-house soldering, testing, and validation. Firmware is developed concurrently using C/C++ or Python, with platforms like STM32, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi Compute Modules.
